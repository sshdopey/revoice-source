Revoice

0.2.4 · Personal Apple Silicon preview

Voice models and settings stay on this Mac. Test recordings stay in memory until you save them to Downloads. Closing a preview discards it. Training is coming soon.

In your call or recording app, choose Revoice Microphone as the microphone. Conversion off sends your natural voice through the same device.

Beatrice 2.0.0-rc.3, local noise reduction and microphone setup are included. No separate installer is needed.

Menu-bar controls

Closing the window or pressing ⌘Q keeps your microphone running. Reopen Revoice from Applications anytime.
